# Patent eligibility and excluded subject matter — CN

Assess the claimed solution as a whole. Identify its technical problem, technical means, technical features, and technical effect. For algorithms, record how algorithmic and technical features support and interact with each other in a specific technical scenario or computer-internal performance improvement.

Flag pure algorithms, business rules, mental activities, diagnosis/treatment methods, unlawful data acquisition, personal-information issues, public-interest issues, and other excluded subject matter. Repeat this assessment against Claims V2.

Primary source: [2026 Patent Examination Guidelines amendment](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2025/11/13/art_99_202568.html).
