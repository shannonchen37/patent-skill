# Final review rules

Against Claims V2, repeat search consistency, eligibility, and unity checks. Validate specification support, priority basis, amendment basis, claim form, abstract, drawings, AI disclosure, terminology, metrics, inventorship contribution mapping, ownership information, disclosure risk, and redaction.

Do not advance a pre-search draft. `CONTENT_READY_FOR_ATTORNEY_REVIEW` means only that the technical content is organized for professional review. `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT` and `DOCX_PACKAGE_RENDERED` are later workflow states; none means filing-ready.
