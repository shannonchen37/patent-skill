# CN drafting workflow

Follow the artifact order in `SKILL.md`. Treat drafting as iterative: analyze a preliminary claim skeleton, draft Claims V1, recheck actual claims, draft the specification, produce Claims V2, then recheck search, eligibility, and unity again. Stop on unresolved facts instead of filling gaps.

`READY_FOR_ATTORNEY_REVIEW` means the review package is organized; it does not mean rights, patentability, or filing compliance have been decided.
