# Inventive step — CN

For the whole claimed solution: identify the closest prior art; determine distinguishing features and their technical effects; formulate the actually solved technical problem without hindsight; then assess teaching, motivation, common knowledge evidence, and the overall combination. Do not ask whether an isolated feature is inventive.

Primary source: [CNIPA guidance on the three-step approach](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2022/2/16/art_2650_173202.html).
