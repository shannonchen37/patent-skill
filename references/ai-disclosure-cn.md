# AI and algorithm disclosure — CN

When relevant, disclose necessary modules/layers/connections, inputs/outputs, training data and steps, necessary parameters, objective function, inference flow, technical meaning of data, scenario relationship, and technical-effect causality. Avoid both unsupported breadth and implementation-only limitations in independent claims.

Primary source: [2026 Patent Examination Guidelines amendment](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2025/11/13/art_99_202568.html).
