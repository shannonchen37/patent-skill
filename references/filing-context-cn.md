# Filing context — CN

Collect inventors and contributions, applicant/ownership facts, public disclosures and dates, prior applications, priority plans, development locations, and foreign-filing plans.

- Do not infer `no disclosure` from missing information.
- Do not assume the six-month novelty exception applies.
- Flag foreign filing where substantial technical content may have been completed in China for confidentiality-review assessment.
- Revisit inventorship and ownership against Claims V2.

Primary sources: [PRC Patent Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2020/11/23/art_524_171347.html), [Implementing Regulations](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2023/12/21/art_98_189197.html).
